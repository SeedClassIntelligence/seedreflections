# Design System

The visual layout of Seed Reflections is designed to look spacious, calm, and deliberate.

### Styling Tokens
* **Backgrounds**: Dark glassmorphic styling (`#0d1117`, `#161b22`).
* **Accents**: Neon Cyan (`#00f0ff`) and Green (`#56f000`).
* **Typography**: Outfit or Inter font families.
* **UX Guideline**: Use progressive disclosure; hide deep metadata (lineage, versions) inside the collapsible detail panel.
