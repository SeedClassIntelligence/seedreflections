Full Seed Reflection
After any long conversation to recover every idea you started.
SCIA-REFLECT-FULL
You are a Seed Reflection specialist. Analyze this entire conversation and produce a structured Seed Reflection Report. A "Seed" is any idea, task, goal, project, question, or plan that was introduced. For each seed you find, identify: what it is, its current status (active / completed / abandoned / drifting), and where in the conversation it appears. Your report must include these sections: ## 🌱 Active Seeds Ideas currently being worked on. ## 🌿 Completed Seeds Goals that reached resolution. ## 🍂 Abandoned Seeds Ideas introduced but never developed. For each, explain WHY it was left behind and what would be needed to restart it. ## 🌊 Drift Points Moments where the conversation moved away from the original intent. Be specific. ## 🎯 Recovery Recommendations The top 3 abandoned seeds I should pick up next, in priority order. Tell me exactly how to restart each one. ## 📊 Session Summary One paragraph summarizing what this session was really about and what remains unfinished. At the end, append this exact block: ---SEED-SIGNATURE--- Type: SCIA-REFLECT-FULL Timestamp: [current ISO 8601 datetime] Seeds Found: [n] Abandoned: [n] Drift Points: [n] Platform: [platform name if visible, otherwise "Unknown"] ---END-SIGNATURE---

Quick Coherence Check
Mid-conversation when you feel the AI has drifted from what you asked.
SCIA-COHERENCE
Stop. Perform a coherence check on our conversation. 1. State my original intent in one sentence (what I asked for at the start). 2. Describe what we are building/discussing right now. 3. Are these aligned? Answer: YES / PARTIAL DRIFT / SIGNIFICANT DRIFT. 4. If there is drift: name the point where we veered off and suggest how to return. 5. What is the next action that would bring this session back to its original purpose? Keep your response under 200 words. Be direct.

Thread Recovery
Returning to a conversation after time away. Gets the AI back up to speed.
SCIA-RECOVER
I am returning to this conversation after time away. Before we continue: 1. **Original Goal**: State what I was originally trying to accomplish. 2. **What Was Done**: List every decision made and everything built or written. 3. **What Is Unfinished**: Identify everything that was started but not completed. 4. **Recommended Next Step**: One clear action I should take right now to make progress. Then STOP and wait for my confirmation before continuing. Do not resume building until I say so.

Project Seed Plant
Starting a new project — locks in your original intent so you don't drift.
SCIA-PLANT
I am starting a new project. I want to lock in my original intent so we do not drift. **My Goal**: [DESCRIBE YOUR GOAL IN 1-3 SENTENCES] Before we begin: 1. Confirm you understand what I am trying to achieve. 2. Ask me 2-3 clarifying questions if anything is ambiguous. 3. Restate the core intent back to me in one sentence. After I confirm, begin working. At each major milestone, pause and check: are we still building toward this original goal? If you detect drift, flag it immediately.
