What Sets Us Apart

Two things no one else offers

These are not feature upgrades. They are entirely new categories.

🌱 Original Seed Recovery

When you start an AI conversation, you arrive with a specific goal — your Original Seed. Sessions drift. Context windows fill. The model loses track. You lose track. Seed Reflections extracts that original intent, scores how far the session drifted, and compiles personalized prompts so you can pick up exactly where you left off — in any new thread, on any platform.

  • Works with exported ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini conversations
  • Accepts pasted transcripts and raw text uploads
  • Generates 7 types of recovery and handoff prompts
  • Stores every seed in your personal Idea Garden

🔏 Seed Signature

Seed Signature is a standalone cryptographic attribution protocol. When you sign a seed, it receives a unique content hash tied to your identity and timestamp. That signature travels with your content everywhere — in pasted text, generated documents, shared links. Anyone can verify it. No one can fake it.

  • Sign any conversation seed with a personal passphrase
  • Receive a permanent, verifiable provenance certificate
  • Embed the signature into any content you share
  • Tamper-detection: any modification invalidates the signature
Competitive Landscape

Who else is in this neighbourhood?

Honestly — nobody does exactly this. But six product categories operate in adjacent spaces. Here's where they end and where we begin.

The gap is real. No product on the market today combines (1) recovering your original intent from a drifted AI conversation, (2) tracking how far it drifted, and (3) cryptographically signing that idea as yours. These are three separate unsolved problems that Seed Reflections addresses as one unified workflow.
Limitless AI
Passive Capture
Wearable AI pendant + software that passively records everything you say in meetings and real-world conversations. Great at total recall of what was spoken. Does not operate on AI chat sessions, cannot recover intent, and signs nothing.
Their Gap
Records everything — recovers nothing specific. Your original seed is still buried inside hours of audio. No signing, no drift detection, no AI session support.
Mem0 / Zep
AI Memory Layer
Developer infrastructure for giving AI agents long-term memory. Mem0 extracts facts and preferences from conversations so AI can remember you over time. Powerful — but built for developers building products, not for individuals managing their own ideas.
Their Gap
Serves the AI's memory, not yours. There's no concept of an Original Seed, no drift tracking, no provenance signing, and no cross-platform portability for end users.
TypingMind
AI Chat Client
A premium multi-model chat interface where you bring your own API keys. Offers better conversation organization than default ChatGPT/Claude interfaces, with folders and prompt libraries. A power user shell — not an intent recovery system.
Their Gap
Better organized chaos, but still chaos. No seed extraction, no coherence scoring, no recovery prompts, no signing. Your original idea still gets lost — it's just in a nicer-looking folder.
Reflect.app
Second Brain
AI-assisted note-taking and knowledge management built around backlinking your thoughts. Great for organizing ideas you've already processed and written down. Not designed for recovering ideas from AI conversations you haven't manually captured.
Their Gap
You have to do the work first. Reflect organizes what you manually bring to it. It won't extract your original intent from a five-hour ChatGPT session or sign anything as yours.
Adobe Content Credentials
Media Provenance
Part of the C2PA standard. Embeds cryptographically signed metadata into image, video, and audio files to prove authorship and track edits. Backed by Adobe, Microsoft, and major camera manufacturers. Built entirely for media files — not text or AI-generated ideas.
Their Gap
Solves provenance for pixels, not for ideas. There is no mechanism for signing a conversation seed, a written concept, or any text-based original intent. Signing a JPEG of your idea is not the same as signing the idea.
Numbers Protocol
Blockchain Provenance
Decentralized digital provenance using blockchain to create a permanent, verifiable ownership record for digital assets. Supports C2PA and extends it with on-chain anchoring. Like Content Credentials — focused entirely on media files, not ideas.
Their Gap
Blockchain-anchored photo and video ownership is powerful — but a text seed from a ChatGPT conversation isn't a media file. No concept of AI session intent, drift, or idea recovery exists in their model.
