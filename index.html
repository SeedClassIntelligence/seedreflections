Proof of Creation

Anyone can copy AI content.
Only you can prove you own the Original Seed.

When you collaborate with AI to generate code, text, or plans — how do you establish provenance? Seed Signature is a cryptographic attribution protocol that stamps your Original Seed (your genesis intent) directly into the generated assets. Anywhere that content is pasted, it carries your cryptographically signed lineage.

✓ Provable Attribution: Every block of output carries your cryptographically signed authorship.
✓ Tamper-Proof Provenance: If anyone edits a single character of your content, the hash mismatches and the signature breaks.
✓ Cross-Platform Portability: Your signed seeds remain verified across Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or GitHub.
